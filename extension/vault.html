Health vault

Everything your watch, scale, cuff and apps measure, next to your glucose. It stays on this computer, and in your own Google Drive if you turn that on.

Whose health data is this?

Your watch, scale and phone measure you. Choose which person you follow is you, the one using this computer. Their learner uses the vault, and pens, calendar and walk heads-ups follow their glucose. Until you choose, the vault counts for nobody.

What su94r learned

From your own readings and markers, and the vault: how much and when each insulin lowers you, what carbs, exercise, walking and sleep do, and how you drift through the day. These are estimates from your past, never a dosing instruction.
