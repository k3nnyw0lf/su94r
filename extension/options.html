su94r Mini

Your FreeStyle Libre reading and graph in a small window on your laptop. Works with FreeStyle Libre through LibreLinkUp.

Moving to su94r Mini

People

    LibreLinkUp accounts

      Add a LibreLinkUp account

      Use a LibreLinkUp login (the follower app). One account can follow many people; care teams can add several accounts.

      First time? Turn on sharing (about 2 minutes)
      1. In the FreeStyle Libre 3 app on the phone of the person wearing the sensor, open the menu, then Connected Apps → LibreLinkUp → Add Connection. Enter the follower email and send the invite.
      2. Install LibreLinkUp (App Store or Google Play), create an account with that email, and accept the invitation and the terms.
      3. Come back here and sign in with that LibreLinkUp email and password.

      Alerts

      Alerts back up the Libre 3 app's own alarms; they do not replace them. If nothing pops up, check Windows Settings → System → Notifications → Google Chrome, and Do not disturb.

      Insulin & medicines

      Log a dose by clicking the graph at the moment you took it (or with the + button). The window then shows your last dose, how long ago it was, and how much rapid insulin is still active, and it warns you before you log a second dose too close to the first.

      Your medicines

          Names come from RxNorm (U.S. National Library of Medicine), free and without an account. Set your usual amount so logging is one click. Insulins added here are logged as insulin, so active insulin and the double-dose warning include them.

          Sensor

          Pens, vials and sensors

          Add the pen or vial you are using and the day you opened it. su94r counts the units you log (plus priming) and tells you before it runs out or reaches its days-after-opening limit. Check your own leaflet: you can change the units and days.

          First choose who uses this computer, on the health vault page. Pens, calendar and walk heads-ups follow that person's glucose.

            Heads-ups

            Calendar: paste your calendar's private iCal link (Google Calendar → Settings → your calendar → "Secret address in iCal format"). Before a meeting, a drive or the gym, su94r tells you if you're low, falling toward low, or high. The link stays on this computer.

            Heat: where you are, for hot-day notes about insulin and so the learner can check what heat does to you (Open-Meteo, free, no sign-up).

            Window

            Keep it on top

            Checking for the pin helper…

            On Windows, the optional pin helper keeps the mini window above every other app for good, even after a restart. Without it, the pin button uses Chrome's float, which needs one click each time. To install it, run pin-helper\install.ps1 from the extension folder (right-click → Run with PowerShell).

            Alexa and screens

            Your own su94r server lets Alexa log and read doses ("Alexa, tell my sugar 4 units of R insulin") and puts your graph on a fridge, TV, tablet or widget. One click connects this computer: su94r Mini shows the server your LibreLinkUp sign-in, and the server gives this computer its own key. Nothing to paste.

              Screens and widgets

              On the fridge, TV or tablet, open su94r.com/tv in its web browser. It shows a 6-character code: type it here and give the screen a name.

                Your computers

                Urgent-low alarm: tick the computers that should sound the loud alarm for an urgent low. The others still show it, quietly. New computers start ticked.

                Markers, doses, your medicine list and alert settings sync between every Chrome browser signed in to the same Google account with Chrome sync on, so a dose logged on one computer counts on the others. Each computer signs in to LibreLinkUp once (sign-ins stay on the device). Edge syncs with other Edge browsers, not with Chrome.

                  Add another computer
                  1. On that computer, open your synced copy of the su94r Mini folder, or download the extension folder from GitHub. Every copy has the same extension ID, wherever the folder is, so they all share one sync.
                  2. Right-click setup-this-pc.ps1 → Run with PowerShell. It opens the extensions page, copies the folder path and installs the pin helper.
                  3. Turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, paste the path (Ctrl+V) and choose Select Folder.
                  4. Sign in to LibreLinkUp once. Markers and settings arrive by themselves, and updates install themselves.

                  History

                  Loading…

                  Every reading is saved in this browser and kept. Each update fills gaps of up to 12 hours on its own (that is all LibreLinkUp keeps). To fill older gaps, download your data from LibreView (libreview.com → sign in → Download glucose data) and import the file here.

                  Delete saved history

                  Removes the saved readings and the AI conversation for the person picked above, from this browser only. Markers and doses are kept: they are shared with your other computers.

                  To delete this person's markers and doses too: those from the last 30 days on all your computers, older ones on this computer.

                  Markers

                  Add meals, insulin and exercise with the + button in a mini window. They show on the graph and in the CSV export. Showing markers for the person picked under History.

                    How to use it

                    • Click the extension's toolbar icon to open the mini window. Pin the icon (puzzle piece → pin) so the number is always visible.
                    • The pin button keeps the window above everything. The shrink button shows just the number; double-click the number to get the graph back.
                    • Hover the graph to read any point or marker. Pick 3 hours up to 7 days.
